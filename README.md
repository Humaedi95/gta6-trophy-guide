**Leonida Trophy Guide** — a guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy has its official name, description and grade, plus a guide written in the author's own words: what to do, where and how, and when it can be missed. The checklist saves in your browser, and the guide works fully offline — open `app/index.html` in desktop Chrome with the network off and everything still loads.

What's inside:

- All 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze.
- A checklist saved in the browser (localStorage), with export (JSON download) and import.
- Filters by kind (story, side, collectible, combat, exploration, activities, progression) and by grade, plus search by name and earned / not-earned filters.
- A spoiler setting that hides story trophies' names and descriptions until you earn them.
- A roadmap to the platinum in nine ordered steps (about 200 hours, difficulty around 4/10 — long, not hard).
- The one missable trophy flagged: **Positive Mental Attitude** — one of its 18 meditation spots is on Lucia's balcony in Chapter 1 and disappears after that chapter, and the game has no mission replay.
- A Sources section that says honestly what is official, what is reported, and what is still uncertain.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Or open `app/index.html` directly in a browser — no build step, no network.

To hand the entry in (what the make.fun scorer reads):

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## Sources

Names, descriptions and grades are the official list as published on PlayStation Network on 18 November 2026 and reported that day by Exophase, PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements. Rockstar had not published a trophy list of its own before launch; difficulty, time estimates and the missable call come from early guide reporting (PowerPyx, GTABase) and may change as players work through the game. The guide text is written in the author's own words — no other guide's text is copied here. No official artwork or logos are used.
