    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty around 4/10 — long, not hard. There are no online trophies, no difficulty trophies and no mission medals. Follow the steps in order; the checklist on the Guide tab tracks every trophy.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list is reported as potentially missable. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story. Because GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

        Sources & honesty notes

        What this list is. The 55 trophy names, descriptions and grades in this guide are the official list as published on PlayStation Network on 18 November 2026, the day before GTA VI launched, and reported that day by Exophase, PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements. Rockstar had not published a trophy list of its own before launch; the names and descriptions come from the PlayStation Network listing as reported by those trackers, and the guide text is written in the author's own words — no other guide's text is copied here.

        What is still uncertain. Difficulty, time estimates and the missable call come from early guide reporting (PowerPyx, GTABase) and may change as players work through the game. The one trophy flagged missable — Positive Mental Attitude — is reported as such because one of its 18 meditation spots is on Lucia's balcony in Chapter 1 and disappears after that chapter. Everything else in this guide is presented as reported, not as confirmed by the author, and will be corrected as launch-era information settles.

        Counts

        55 trophies on PlayStation 5: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. The same 55 exist as Xbox achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore. 27 trophies are visible from the start and 28 are hidden until earned. All of them are single-player and offline.
